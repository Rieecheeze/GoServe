## 1. What GoServe provides

GoServe helps authorized users view campus office information, submit service requests, track their progress, communicate with offices, arrange available consultations, review eligible payments and receive requested documents. Seryo is the app's optional AI assistant.

The current project is a sandbox demonstration for TIP Manila's Casal and Arlegui campuses. Names, records, schedules and fees marked as demonstrations are test content. They must not be presented as official school records, confirmed school policies or real financial transactions. Real funds and production student records must not be used in the demonstration unless a separately authorized pilot establishes the applicable arrangements first.

GoServe does not independently change official enrollment, grades, academic standing or school financial records. A request reference, chat message or AI response does not itself establish that the institution has approved a service or received payment.
