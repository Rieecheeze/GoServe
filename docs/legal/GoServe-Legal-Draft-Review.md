## Proposed presentation in the app

1. Make Terms and the Privacy Notice readable before account completion, including large text and accessible scrolling.
2. Use a deliberate, initially unselected Terms agreement control: “I agree to the GoServe Terms and Conditions.” Reading or scrolling is not acceptance.
3. Link the Privacy Notice separately. A notice explains data practices; it is not consent by itself, as explained by [NPC Advisory Opinion No. 2018-058](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/AONo_2018-058.pdf).
4. If a specific processing purpose requires consent, collect that purpose-specific choice separately. Do not force optional AI consent into registration.
5. Keep AI controls separate: an approved provider-specific notice, Allow / Not now, and later withdrawal through AI privacy. Allow must not send data; Send remains explicit.
6. Preserve the accepted document version and server acceptance time, with read-only access to the relevant Terms. Material changes need a deliberate version/reacceptance policy.
7. Do not publish a draft, insert a demo acceptance against the six accounts or mark any user's Terms accepted automatically.
