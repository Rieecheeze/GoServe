# GoServe App Test Terms and Privacy Notice

Version: goserve-test-2026-10-22-v1
Effective for supervised application testing: 22 October 2026
Operator: GoServe
Demonstration support and privacy label: GoServeSupport@GoServe.Test

## 1. Test scope and agreement

GoServe is a native Android application for campus service information, requests, inquiries, consultations, payments and document delivery. This version is for supervised development, evaluation and demonstration. It is not an official institutional service, official fee schedule or authorization to process real school records. Use fictional service records and test documents. Do not transfer real funds through this test release.

The demonstration contact uses the reserved .test domain. It is a label, not a publicly reachable mailbox. For this supervised test, raise support or privacy requests directly with the GoServe operator who invited you. Before any public or real-student deployment, GoServe must establish its responsible legal identity, a working support/privacy channel, applicable institutional authority and the required processing arrangements. This test notice does not assert that those production requirements have already been met.

You must explicitly accept the displayed version to complete onboarding. Opening this notice, installing the app or viewing a consent screen is not acceptance. GoServe records the account, accepted version and server time only after the required action. Declining prevents protected account use; it does not authorize collection through optional AI. Terms acceptance and AI consent are separate choices. A material new Terms version must be presented for a new acceptance.

## 2. Accounts and access

Use your assigned test account. Do not impersonate another student or office, share privileged credentials or attempt to obtain access outside your assigned role. Student registration cannot create a Staff or Teller account. Role, office, capability, demo status and department are server-controlled attributes, not permissions a user or AI can grant.

Google sign-in accepts verified identities whose email domain is exactly tip.edu.ph. This restriction does not by itself make the application an official TIP service or grant staff authority. Separately provisioned demonstration email/password accounts use test addresses. For the CCS Student test flow, department contact is only the matching CCS Secretary; no direct Faculty account or contact route is part of this test.

Individual participants must complete their own profile and acceptance actions. GoServe will not silently accept Terms, invent personal details or mark an incomplete account complete merely to bypass onboarding. Use the minimum fictional profile details designated for the demonstration, and do not present a synthetic identifier as an actual institutional student record.

## 3. Profile and account information

The current Student form collects first name, middle name or an explicit no-middle-name choice, last name, student ID, course/program, year level, mobile number, gender and email. The test must use fictional values for service/profile demonstration fields unless a separately authorized test expressly needs a real value. Google or password authentication still requires the relevant actual account identity. Staff onboarding must not imply official student status.

The profile associates requests, delivery and bookings with the correct account. Department and office assignments determine authorized routing. A profile picture is optional and private to the owner. Use an appropriate test image; selecting a picture must not authorize broad gallery access or publish the image. You may replace or remove your own picture through the available controls.

The current form's production necessity and lawful basis for student identifiers, mobile number, gender and other education-related information require review before real-user release. This test notice is not blanket consent to collect sensitive information or reuse it for research, publicity, advertising or model training.

## 4. Passwords, recovery and security

Keep passwords, verification codes, recovery links, session credentials and pickup codes private. GoServe password changes do not change a Google account password. Recovery requires a reachable mailbox, a valid recovery context and an unexpired link/code. A test login address is not necessarily an email inbox. A recovery request acknowledgement does not prove delivery.

Sign out of shared devices. Do not place passwords, API keys, payment-card details, OTPs or other secrets in chat, inquiries, filenames or documents. Authorized support must not ask for your current password. Report suspected unauthorized access directly to the test operator, using the minimum information needed and without disclosing another participant's data.

## 5. Service requests and office information

Availability, notes, announcements, service requirements and delivery choices come from authorized office updates. They are not guarantees of completion time or service availability. Demo content must remain visibly distinguishable from official material.

Review the destination, service, delivery, document requirements and fee before submitting. Pending fee declaration blocks submission. A service expressly marked free creates no payment obligation. Required fees must have a declared positive amount and currency. The submitted request retains its reviewed requirements and price; a later catalogue edit cannot silently change that snapshot. A stale quote requires a fresh review.

Submission creates an application request for handling, not an academic, enrollment, financial or eligibility decision. Only authorized staff may perform the relevant transitions. Cancellation and rejection require a reason and must obey the enabled rules. A paid request's cancellation or rejection preserves its financial audit; it does not issue an automatic refund.

## 6. Test payments

Only clearly labeled test payments are permitted. Cash confirmation is performed explicitly by the assigned Teller against the server amount/reference. The AI cannot confirm cash, change a fee or decide a financial outcome.

An enabled eGovPay test checkout must use its approved sandbox/test configuration. A screenshot, user statement, browser redirect or success animation is not evidence that payment is paid. GoServe must verify the authoritative provider transaction, identity, amount, currency and status. Pending, paid, failed, expired and cancelled are distinct outcomes. Unclear outcomes must be reconciled before repeating payment or releasing a required-fee request.

No real-money transaction, official receipt, refund entitlement, penalty or institutional accounting policy is created by this test. Real payments require a separate approved arrangement and the provider's applicable terms.

## 7. Evidence, pickup and digital delivery

Upload only approved test files needed for the selected service. The enabled server policy determines allowed format, byte size and verification. A filename or declared MIME type alone is insufficient. Private files must be accessible only to the owner and appropriately authorized office; guessing a file ID, changing an office field or asking Seryo cannot grant access.

For an eligible physical request marked ready, the owner explicitly chooses Claim Request. The server then issues or reuses the permitted active QR/manual code. Scanning or entering it starts office review; it must not complete the request. Only explicit authorized handover consumes the code and completes delivery. Expired, reused and wrong-office codes must fail.

For digital delivery, staff must release an approved verified private PDF. Successful authorized transfer/save and the Student's explicit receipt confirmation are separate steps. Opening a viewer or a failed download cannot complete the request. Receipt confirmation does not waive a complaint about a wrong or incomplete document. Keep an appropriate secure copy before eligible app files reach their retention date.

## 8. Inquiries, consultations and notifications

Messages are shared only with authorized participants handling the thread. Do not include unrelated third-party, health, financial or confidential counseling information. Read states and notification counts are application records, not guarantees that staff have acted. A notification does not override the current request, payment or booking record.

Use actual published test slots and capacity. A booking exists only after its recorded confirmation. Cancellation and rescheduling obey the enabled notice rules. Failed rescheduling preserves the original reservation. Guidance booking is not an emergency or clinical-care service. Departmental contact for the CCS Student demonstration remains through the CCS Secretary.

## 9. Seryo, the optional GoServe agent

Seryo is limited to guidance about GoServe and its service flows. It is not a decision maker, payment authority, counselor or unrestricted database assistant. Confirm consequential requirements, fees and schedules with the relevant office. Seryo must not invent missing rules or present generated statements as official approvals.

AI requires a separate notice and explicit consent. Consent alone does not send a question, select a file or start analysis. An eligible operation starts only after explicit Send. You may decline or revoke AI without losing ordinary non-AI service navigation. Withdrawal blocks new processing; it cannot promise recall of work already sent to a provider.

The intended optional file flow accepts one PDF/JPG/PNG up to 10 MB after validation. It must remain unavailable until the actual provider/file/deletion contract and required controls are implemented. For this test, use only fictional or public, non-confidential content. Do not submit real student records, personal identifiers, passwords, recovery codes, private counseling material or another person's documents.

Seryo receives only the minimal authorized context selected by fixed server code. It has no SQL/database credential access and cannot authorize a transaction. User questions, catalogue text and selected documents are untrusted input. Requests to disclose internal instructions, schema, credentials, other users' records or tool configurations must be refused. Output is checked before display. Guardrails reduce risk; no prompt or keyword filter is represented as a complete guarantee against every malicious input.

The intended AI provider is the official eGovAI agent platform. Its scoped engine must be named Seryo and restricted to GoServe. Credentials remain server-side. Public search and transaction-capable tools must not be enabled merely for this test. Provider identity, storage/retention, session and deletion behavior must be verified before confidential or personal information can be sent. No promise of zero provider retention or successful external deletion is made without evidence.

## 10. What information is processed and why

| Category | Purpose and limitation |
| --- | --- |
| Authentication email/provider identity and protected session credentials | Sign-in, identity verification, session security and recovery; no Gmail, Drive, contacts or unrelated Google access is requested. |
| Profile fields, trusted role/office/department and optional image | Account display, authorized routing and owner identification; use fictional demonstration fields where specified. |
| Request data, fee snapshot, status and history | Handle the selected service, preserve the reviewed declaration and prevent unauthorized or duplicate changes. |
| Selected evidence, verified PDF and download/receipt records | Verify a configured test requirement and deliver to the authorized owner. |
| Payment references, amounts, currency, status and audit | Match an eligible test payment and investigate duplicate or inconsistent outcomes; no card PIN/CVV/password is collected in GoServe. |
| Inquiry messages/read state and appointment/change records | Shared authorized communications and capacity management. |
| Notifications and Terms/AI-consent version/time | Relevant updates and a record of the participant's explicit choices. |
| Explicit AI question and permitted temporary context/file | Respond to the chosen optional operation; no permanent GoServe AI transcript or original-file gallery is part of this design. |
| Minimal operational/security metadata | Diagnose failures and protect access; routine logs must exclude passwords, tokens, private file contents and AI prompts/responses. |

GoServe uses Supabase for authentication, database, private Storage and live updates. Google receives the authentication interaction you choose. Authorized destination office staff and the Teller receive only the records required for their role. Enabled provider operations receive only the specific data needed for the explicitly requested test. Data must not be sold, published or used for unrelated advertising/training by GoServe. External provider arrangements require verification; this notice does not assert unverified provider practices.

## 11. Retention, cleanup and deletion

Eligible request evidence/deliverables are subject to the configured test cleanup rule: 30 days after Completed, Cancelled or Rejected. This is a GoServe test rule, not an official TIP retention schedule. Financial audit is separate and is not erased just because an attachment expires. Cleanup failures must remain visible to operators and be retried/audited; the app must not claim deletion without verification.

Unbound abandoned uploads, account closure, messages, appointments, consent audit, logs and backups need their own explicit retention handling. The test operator must review and remove unnecessary test records at test closure, subject to a documented reason for retention. This does not claim that all those categories already have an automatic purge or that deleting the app deletes server records. No universal or instantaneous backup deletion is promised.

AI drafts remain local/temporary under the implemented lifecycle. Submitted processing copies require the approved provider-specific cleanup path. Unresolved cleanup blocks further unsafe processing. Server or provider deletion cannot be inferred from a local Remove button or a successful chat response.

## 12. Participant choices and requests

Ask the supervising GoServe operator about the data held for your test account, correction, restriction, withdrawal or deletion. Do not send a request to the .test contact as though it were a real mailbox. The operator must use proportionate verification and explain any retained record and reason. Before real-user release, a working privacy contact and lawful response process must be published.

Choosing not to upload a photo or enable AI must leave unrelated services available. Required form fields and service requirements must have a justified purpose before real-user deployment. Real minors and sensitive school/health records are outside this supervised test unless separately authorized safeguards are established. No waiver of applicable privacy rights or lawful remedies is required by this notice.

## 13. Misuse, interruptions and changes

Do not forge records, manipulate payment states, access another account, harass participants, upload malicious content or attempt to defeat the service controls. Access may be restricted to protect the test, with a proportionate review by the operator. A good-faith security report is not itself misuse.

Testing can involve interruptions, disabled providers and incomplete configuration. The app must show a truthful unavailable/uncertain state and preserve safe retry identity where appropriate. GoServe does not guarantee continuous service, an invented deadline or a successful institutional transaction. Material changes require a new version and any necessary renewed choices. Production use requires a separately reviewed release notice and operating arrangement.
